Detailed Material Case Studies

Case Study 101: Reclaimed Cypress Siding

Installed across our Timber Pavilion project, these cypress boards were reclaimed from decommissioned local grain silos. The timber was pressure-treated using natural plant oils to retain weather resistance without environmental run-off.

Case Study 102: Ultra-High Performance Concrete (UHPC)

For the Concrete Balcony project, UHPC allowed thin profile cantilevers that reduce overall dead load weight by 40% while doubling structural shear resistance against high winds.
