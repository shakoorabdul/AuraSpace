Our Architectural Philosophies

At Aura Space, we approach spatial planning as an equilibrium between environmental raw context and human behavior patterns. We select timeless materials that mature gracefully alongside the families living inside our geometric silhouettes.

The Brutalist Pavilion

A structural residence balancing raw structural poured concrete modules with custom-crafted teak accents nestled along a forested hillside line.

The Lumina Atrium

An urban townhome refurbishment focusing entirely on maximizing daylight harvesting channels via geometric skylight insertions.

Our Design Methodology

Every project starts with environmental impact modeling and lifecycle analysis. We integrate passive cooling, renewable materials, and low-VOC finishes into every architectural document.

1. Spatial Discovery

Detailed analysis of sun angles, local materials, and functional spatial requirements.

2. Parametric Drafting

Generating adaptive floorplans to optimize usable floor area and minimize waste.

3. Sustainable Build

Executing construction using locally sourced stone, timber, and recycled metals.
